## Purpose
The purpose of this skill is to equip architects with the ability to write clear, concise, and compliant building specifications that guide the construction process.

## When to Use
Use this skill when preparing detailed drawings and specifications for building projects, consulting with clients, and ensuring compliance with relevant regulations.

## Instructions
1. Gather project requirements and client preferences through consultations.
2. Research relevant building codes and zoning laws applicable to the project.
3. Create detailed drawings and specifications using appropriate design software (e.g., AutoCAD, Revit).
4. Ensure the specifications clearly define materials, methods, and quality standards.
5. Review the specifications with clients and stakeholders for feedback.
6. Revise the specifications based on feedback and ensure compliance with legal requirements.
7. Finalize the documents and prepare for project handover to contractors.
