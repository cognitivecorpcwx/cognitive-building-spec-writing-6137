# Industry Standards and Best Practices

## Building Codes
- International Building Code (IBC)
- National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) Standards

## Specification Writing Standards
- Construction Specifications Institute (CSI) MasterFormat
- National Institute of Building Sciences (NIBS) guidelines

## Best Practices
1. Always validate specifications against current regulations.
2. Use clear and non-ambiguous language.
3. Involve stakeholders early in the specification writing process.
